Scoreward Privacy Policy

This policy covers personal-information handling across the Scoreward app, including personal records, game progress, backups, purchases, and related permissions.

Scope and responsibility

This policy explains how Scoreward handles personal records, game progress, backups, purchases, and permission-related information. The provider responsible for this app is 大熊猫侯佩|PandaHopy. Support and privacy contact: 19796174@qq.com. Apple and any email, file-storage, or sharing service you choose handle their own services under their respective policies.

On-device records and purposes

The app can store the tasks, goals, descriptions, journals, assessments, timer and counter records, signatures, and custom names you enter, along with relevant dates, time zones, game progress, and assets. This information supports your records, game rules, reflection, and recovery. Language, appearance, and reminder settings are local preferences. Enter only what is needed; avoid identity-document numbers, payment passwords, or unnecessary private information about others.

Backups, iCloud, and exports

You can back up or export complete game records through the relevant features. Files may include tasks, journals, signatures, and transaction or asset records. When you enable or enter iCloud backup features, the app uses Apple's iCloud services to look up, download, or save backups, which may transfer data over a network. New exported backups use encryption and integrity checks to reduce casual reading and detect tampering. No user password is required, and other compatible app installations can restore the files, so this is not end-to-end encryption with a key held only by you. Earlier backups may still be plain text; keep or delete them carefully.

Purchases, subscriptions, and security records

Apple handles payments and related account information. The app does not need your payment-card number or Apple Account password. It verifies Apple's signed transactions and locally stores necessary facts such as product and transaction identifiers, dates, environment, delivery, paid subscription coverage, and wallet events. Recognition of earlier purchases and duplicate-claim prevention may retain a hash of an Apple app transaction identifier and minimal Keychain records. These may still associate purchases and are not fully anonymous. The current version does not validate purchases on an app-operated server; Apple services can still require a network connection.

Permissions and local reminders

Notifications are off by default and are enabled by your choice. Based on your settings, the app schedules local reminders for goal deadlines, pet outings, training, and daily assessments. Notification text does not include task, hero, or pet names. iOS permissions, Focus, and system policies determine delivery and presentation. Features such as saving a courtyard postcard request photo-write permission when needed; profile-photo capture or selection uses the relevant system flow. You can decline optional permissions and continue using unrelated features, and change permissions in system settings.

Sharing and contacting support

Exporting, sharing, saving images, or emailing support may provide the content you choose to Apple or another selected service. Check for private records before sharing. To respond to support requests, we use the email address, problem description, and necessary diagnostics you choose to provide. You do not need to send passwords, verification codes, or a complete private backup. If more information is needed, we will explain its scope and purpose. Contacting support is not permission for marketing or future AI use.

AI and other processing

AI features are disabled in the current release, and using the app does not require sending records to an AI service. Retained AI implementation code does not authorize current uploads. Before enabling such features, we will explain the provider, data, and purpose and obtain separate permission where required. This terms confirmation does not authorize cross-app tracking, advertising profiles, or new third-party sharing.

Retention, deletion, and device changes

Local records support app features while you continue using and keeping them. You can manage on-device data through available delete or new-journey tools and system controls. Irreversible deletion requires a separate explanation. Removing the app generally removes its local sandbox data, but does not automatically delete iCloud or exported files, Apple's purchase records, or necessarily all security records in Keychain. It also does not cancel subscriptions. Manage backups separately in the relevant file service. Support emails and attachments are retained only for the shortest period needed to handle your request, related disputes, or legal obligations. When retention is no longer necessary, we will delete or de-identify them. You can also request deletion by email; if legal retention is still necessary, we will explain why.

Choices and requests

You can change language, turn off optional permissions, manage your records and backups, manage subscriptions through your Apple Account, and contact us to request access, correction, deletion, or other applicable data rights. We will respond within the scope of applicable law and our actual control, explaining necessary identity checks or lawful retention rather than asking for unrelated identity documents. Withdrawing confirmation of necessary terms may return the app to its explanation screen; it does not automatically delete records or cancel subscriptions. Contact support if you still need access to existing data. Getting help should not require buying again.

Safeguards and limits

The app uses platform storage protection, signed-transaction verification, and backup integrity checks to reduce unintended access and duplicate delivery. No system can guarantee absolute security. Someone with an exported file, an unlocked device, or technical tools may still access information. Protect your device, system accounts, and backups, and do not share verification codes or purchase credentials. Encryption is not a guarantee that information can never be exposed.

Policy website hosting

This policy website is hosted by GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub logs and stores visitor IP addresses and related technical access information for security purposes; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. Website access is separate from storage of on-device app records. Opening this website alone does not upload tasks, journals, or backups from the app to this site.

Policy updates will identify the relevant version and effective date. Material changes or new data purposes will be explained and require permission where applicable.
